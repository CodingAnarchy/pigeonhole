### Changed
- **`CpuProfile::Embedded`, the default, has each shard thread size its poll for the next commit from its recent gaps between commits: 200 µs, up to 1 ms when commits come steadily** (#1176). The window is the longest whose expected poll per gap is no more than a fixed 200 µs window's, so a shard never polls more per gap than before. `CpuProfile::Server` keeps its fixed 1 ms, and an idle database still never polls. On the reference machine, sparse-wide's default-durability put p99 fell from 390 to 122 µs, and its `Buffered` put p99 from 38 µs to `Server`'s 27 µs. ycsb-a paced to 1K and 4K ops/s used the same CPU as before, and at 20K 0.98 cores against 0.65 (`Server`: 3.5). Under full load the shard threads poll longer, for about one more core.
