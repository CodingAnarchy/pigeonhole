### Added
- Half-precision vector families (#1263, ICR 0104): `Element::F16` and `Element::BF16` under L2, inner product and cosine store each component in 2 bytes. Write raw bit patterns with `put_vector_f16`/`put_vector_bf16`, or `f32` vectors with `put_vector_f32`, which rounds to nearest-even and refuses a component that overflows (or a cosine vector that rounds to zero). Queries stay `f32`: `nearest_f32` and `VectorSpec::distance_f32` score a half vector exactly as its widened `f32` vector. `VectorRef::to_f32_vec` widens, and `Vector::from_f16_bits`/`from_bf16_bits` make owned ones. In Python, `Element.F16`/`Element.BF16` families take `f32` numbers or buffers, and read back widened to `array('f')`.
