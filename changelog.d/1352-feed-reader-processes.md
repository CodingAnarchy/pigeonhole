### Added
- Reader processes can follow the change feed (D310, #1248): `ReadTable::feed()` gives the same `Feed` a writer's `Table::feed()` does, through the writer's shared memory. The writer does no per-commit work for it. Across a writer restart the feed resumes from its own token on the new writer: a durable feed continues, and a visible one that delivered what a power loss took expires. `FeedBuilder::read_table` adds more tables. Every start works there, `from_oldest` and `from_snapshot` included, and a start whose history retention already recycled expires rather than skipping it.
### Changed
- The shared-memory layout is now version 8, which adds each shard's change-feed floor and checkpoint for reader processes. A writer and a reader process from builds on different layouts refuse each other cleanly, never misreading: a reader opening against a 1.0.x writer, or a 1.0.x reader against this writer, fails with `ShmVersionMismatch`. Upgrade the writer and its reader processes together (restart the readers after the writer), as for every earlier layout change.
