### Added
- `phdb serve` serves `phdb mcp`'s data tools as remote MCP, over Streamable HTTP at `/mcp` (D313 ticket 8c-3a). The tools and their answers are `phdb mcp`'s, and every call passes the server's own rules: the token's scope (a read token isn't offered the write tools), a replica's write refusal, the in-flight and concurrency limits, and the reserved `__phdb_` prefix. Sessions (`Mcp-Session-Id`) are bound to their token, with new flags for their leases, their concurrent requests and their cursor cache (`--mcp-session-idle-ms`, `--mcp-session-lifetime-ms`, `--mcp-session-requests`, `--mcp-session-cache-bytes`), and `--mcp-max-result-bytes` and `--mcp-max-value-bytes` for results.
