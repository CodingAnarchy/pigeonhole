### Added
- **`phdb serve`: `Vectors.BulkLoad`** (D313, ICR 0100 and 0112, #1397): a client stream of vectors loaded into an empty IVF index, over gRPC and as NDJSON over HTTP. Each message holds its share of `--inflight-bytes` until the load takes it, so a slow load holds back its client. One load runs at a time, on a thread of its own off the shards' cores, and `/metrics` shows its progress. What a load holds while it clusters its sample and fills its lists is bounded by the new `--bulk-load-bytes` (1 GiB): a load whose worst case is more, as `expected` sets it, is refused before it starts, naming the bytes it needs. A load that sends more than twice what `expected`'s lists hold before they split stops there, the vectors before it loaded. A load whose client sends nothing for `--stream-idle-ms` ends there too. The background rebalancer threads now run off the shards' cores too.
