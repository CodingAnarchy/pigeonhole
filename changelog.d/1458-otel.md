### Added
- `pigeonhole-otel`, a new crate on its own 0.N version line, exports a database's engine metrics through OpenTelemetry (D316, #1458, ICR 0115). `register(&db, &meter)` adds them to an application's `Meter`, for Prometheus, Grafana or an OTLP backend.
  - **The metrics:** commits by durability and engine-side commit latency, transactions, write stalls, flushes and compactions, the block and row caches, file and SST sizes, readers and threads, io_uring, the change feed's retention, and health.
  - **The contract:** their `pigeonhole.*` names, kinds, units and attributes are stable, and `pigeonhole_otel::metrics()` lists them.
  - **The cost:** every instrument is observable, so commits and reads pay nothing.
  - **The docs:** the [observability guide](docs/guide/observability.md) covers wiring, what to alert on and cardinality. A starter Grafana dashboard is `docs/grafana/pigeonhole.json`.
- `EngineMetrics::commit_latency(durability)` and `LatencyHistogram`: commit latency as a histogram, so two readings give an interval's quantiles, which `CommitStats`' since-open percentiles can't (ICR 0115).
- `Pigeonhole::path()` and `PigeonholeReader::path()`: the path as given to the open. `Pigeonhole::is_closed()` (ICR 0115).
