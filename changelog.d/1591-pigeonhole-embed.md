### Added
- **`pigeonhole-embed` 0.1** (#1345, D318, ICR 0119): local embeddings for `pigeonhole-memory`. `LocalEmbedder` runs `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` at a pinned revision on the CPU, in-process, with no embedding service. `embed_passages` is for what you remember, `embed_query` for what you recall by, and `embedding_model()` gives the id a store pins (`BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5@5c38ec7c`). The model's files are downloaded once from Hugging Face into a cache (or loaded from a directory, or offline only), and every file is checked against a SHA-256 pinned in the crate. The crate is on its own 0.N version line.
