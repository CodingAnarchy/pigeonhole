### Fixed
- A backup (`Pigeonhole::backup`, `backup_durable`, `backup_at`, `phdb backup`, `Admin.BackupDownload` and a replica's bootstrap) is about the size of what it holds again. Each SST it wrote from memtables, or rewrote without commits above its snapshot, took a 64 MiB-aligned slot of the copy, so a small database with several families made a copy of hundreds of MiB, nearly all free (#1595).
