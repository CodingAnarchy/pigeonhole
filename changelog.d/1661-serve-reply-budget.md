### Changed
- **`phdb serve` counts unary read replies against `--inflight-bytes`** (#1567). `ReadRow`, `MultiGet`, `Graph.Traverse`, `Vectors.Nearest` and `Memory.Recall` reserve their reply's cap before reading (twice that for HTTP/JSON), and `Get` its cell's size after. A built reply keeps only its own size until it has been written. A read whose share isn't free within `--request-timeout-ms` now gets `Overloaded` (retry-safe, nothing read). A reply whose client takes nothing of it for `--stream-idle-ms` is dropped, and its share freed. New metrics: `pigeonhole_serve_inflight_reply_bytes` and `pigeonhole_serve_reply_idle_drops_total`.
