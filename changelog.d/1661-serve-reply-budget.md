### Added
- **`phdb serve` counts unary read replies against `--inflight-bytes`** (#1567). A read asking for more than the default page (1 MiB) reserves its `page_bytes` before reading (twice that for HTTP/JSON), so `--inflight-bytes` ÷ `page_bytes` caps how many build at once. A read within the default page, `Get` and `Memory.Recall` are charged their reply's size once built, and a reply of 64 KiB or less takes nothing. A built reply keeps only its own size until it has been written. A read whose share isn't free within `--request-timeout-ms` gets `Overloaded` (retry-safe, nothing read). A reply whose client takes nothing of it for `--stream-idle-ms` is dropped, and its share freed. New metrics: `pigeonhole_serve_inflight_reply_bytes`, `pigeonhole_serve_reply_idle_drops_total` and `pigeonhole_serve_reply_rereads_total`.
