### Changed
- `phdb serve`: while every `--max-catch-up` slot is busy, a change-feed subscription keeps a slow stream's place on its ring instead of pushing it into the catch-up queue, and reads no further from the database until the stream takes its events, a slot frees, the stream has taken nothing for a second, or the subscription is half the feed's history behind (#1726). At saturation, subscribers lag the database instead of losing their place, and writes never wait for it. `/metrics` gains `pigeonhole_serve_feed_paced_waits_total`, `pigeonhole_serve_feed_paced_hubs` and `pigeonhole_serve_feed_paced_released_total{reason}`.
