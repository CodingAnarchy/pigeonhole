### Fixed
- `phdb serve`: on Linux with pinned shards, the network workers also run on the shards' SMT siblings, never on a shard's own CPU. So puts no longer wait for a CPU behind feed delivery: at 16 table sets × 16 feed streams the masked server held puts to about 33K of 55K puts/s (#1734). The feed and feed hub threads, the blocking threads (scans, `ReadRow`, `/mcp` and the other sync calls) and the background threads stay on the free cores. The startup line names the siblings.
