### Fixed
- `pigeonhole-vector`: a split no longer reads a list whole. A list that grew past four times its split length (a bulk load whose vectors mostly fell in one list, or writes while a background rebalancer was behind) was read into memory whole by its split, and phase 2 read each neighbour whole; now such a list is split from a sample and moved a page per transaction, and phase 2 reads a page at a time, so a split holds at most four lists' vectors (#1824). `phdb serve`'s rebalance after a `Vectors.BulkLoad` no longer needs memory outside `--bulk-load-bytes` for a skewed load.
