### Fixed
- **With `Options::auto_recover`, `commit_returning_async` reported a failed engine's refusal as its raw error** (#1847). Every other commit form, `commit_if_returning_async` included, reports a commit the failed engine refused before logging anything as `Busy` ("not committed, retry on the recovered one") once the recovery swapped the engine in. `commit_returning_async` returned the engine's own error instead (an I/O error telling the caller to reopen the database, or `Closed`), so a caller couldn't tell whether its increment landed. It now reports it as the other forms do. Present since 1.1.0.
