### Changed
- A WAL rollover no longer syncs or grows the stream file on the shard thread when no spare slot is ready (#19, D200): `Wal::blocked` also holds the engine's group back while a spare is prepared, and the preparation wakes the shard. Only counted fallbacks remain (a group larger than a segment's last quarter, a failed preparation). `SpareSegments::prepare` zero-fills blank slots instead of counting them as spares. `pigeonhole-wal` adds `StrictForeground`: inside it, debug builds panic if `append`, `write` or `submit_sync` blocks on a sync outside those fallbacks; the engine wraps its group commit in it.
