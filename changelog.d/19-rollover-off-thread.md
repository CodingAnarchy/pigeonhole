### Changed
- A WAL segment rollover no longer waits on the shard thread for the full segment's sync (#19, D200): only the successor's header waits for it, and the successor's records are written meanwhile (FORMAT §10.1 rule 1 now says they may be). A Buffered commit in that window is acknowledged once its header is written, through a sync. `Wal::blocked` and `Wal::notify_unblocked` (defaulted) let the engine hold a group back in the rare case a segment fills while its own header still waits; `WalCounters::rollover_blocks`/`rollover_waits` and `Metrics::wal_rollover_blocks` count it. A Buffered ticket is now also met once its record is durable.
