### Changed
- A shard prepares its WAL spare slots in short steps (D35): the zero-fill writes and the sync are submitted to the I/O backend, and the background task waits for them blocked, instead of allocating, zero-filling and syncing a whole slot (64 MiB by default) in one step on the shard thread. That step held a shard for about 30 ms on the Phase 3 reference machine. `pigeonhole-wal` adds `SpareSegments::start`, which returns a `SparePrep` future that allocates at most one slot and submits at most four 1 MiB writes per poll; `SpareSegments::prepare` stays for callers with a thread of their own.
