### Fixed
- **A reader process's feed of every table expires once the writer's change-feed sidecar can no longer be written** (#1869, D315 amendment). Before, after a failed sidecar append or sync, such a feed in a reader process waited at the next catalog change until the writer restarted, delivering nothing and reporting nothing. It now expires as one in the writer's process does, with the same reason, and the subscriber resubscribes from its token. The writer publishes the stop in its shared-memory header (offset 208, `feed_flags`); the layout version is unchanged, and a 1.2 reader process ignores it and waits as before.
