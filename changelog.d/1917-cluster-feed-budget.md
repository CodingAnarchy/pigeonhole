### Changed
- `phdb serve --cluster`: `--change-feed` is the node's total, split across its partitions by their shards, as `--engine-memory` is (#1786). In 1.2, each partition kept the whole amount. A cluster node now keeps about each partition's shard share of the feed history per partition that 1.2 kept (1/P with P partitions of equal shards), and writes that share of what 1.2 wrote at start. To keep as much, start with `--change-feed` × the node's shards ÷ the smallest partition's shards (× P with equal shards). On the first 1.3 start, a subscriber or token further back than the history now kept gets `FeedExpired` once; the start logs each partition whose history shrinks. A partition opened while the node serves keeps at least the history it came with (a move) until the node restarts. A single node is unchanged.
