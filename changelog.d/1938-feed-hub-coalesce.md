### Changed
- `phdb serve`: a change-feed hub tells its streams about new commits once per batch it reads, not once per commit, which takes fewer wakes under steady writes.
- `phdb serve`: new `--feed-hub-coalesce-ticks N` (default 1) and `--feed-client-coalesce-ticks N` (default 0). After a small batch, a hub or a stream waits N 1 ms ticks before looking again, so the commits arriving meanwhile are read together: fewer wakes per commit for a few milliseconds more lag under steady writes. A commit after a quiet gap is never held back (#1901).
- `phdb serve`: **the hub default of 1 tick trades lag for CPU.** On the reference machine (g114, 16 table sets × 16 streams, 2 hub threads), CPU per delivered event fell 42% at 20K puts/s and 39% at 2K. Feed lag p99 rose to about 3.5 ms at 20K puts/s and 2.2–2.8 ms at 2K. `--feed-hub-coalesce-ticks 0` gives the lowest lag (p99 0.7 ms at 20K puts/s).
