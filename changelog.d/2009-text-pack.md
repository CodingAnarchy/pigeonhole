### Added
- Text search: `Index::pack` moves an index's postings into blocks, and a top-10 search of a common word on a packed index is several times faster (about 5 times on a 100,000-document index, one laptop). Results and scores are unchanged. **Packing makes an index unreadable by 1.2: pack only once you won't roll back.** Nothing packs an index unless you call `pack`, and the first pack is refused while reader processes are attached unless `PackOptions::with_readers(true)`. Also new: `Index::pack_with` and `pack_pending`, `PackStats`, `IndexStats::packed` and `tombstones`, and `Error::PackRefused` (#1585, ICR 0143).
