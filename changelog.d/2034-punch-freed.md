### Added
- `Options::punch_freed(true)` gives the data file's free space back to the filesystem on Linux and macOS (D333, #2013). Off by default. After compaction frees space, a background pass punches holes in it, one block at a time and paced, keeping the lowest free space written for new data. A database whose data shrank after large deletes or TTL expiry then occupies about its live data on disk (`du`), though its length (`ls -l`) stays the same. Only space that nothing can still read or recover is punched. New `EngineMetrics` fields: `file_punches`, `file_punch_nanos`, `file_punch_bytes`, `file_punched_bytes` (a gauge), `file_punch_passes` and `file_punch_refusals`. `pigeonhole-otel` and `phdb serve`'s `/metrics` export them as `pigeonhole.file.punches`, `pigeonhole.file.punch.time`, `pigeonhole.file.punched`, `pigeonhole.file.holes`, `pigeonhole.file.punch.passes` and `pigeonhole.file.punch.refusals`.
