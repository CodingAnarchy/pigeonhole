### Added
- `Options::punch_freed(true)` gives the data file's free space back to the filesystem on Linux and macOS (D333, #2013). Off by default. After compaction frees space, a background pass punches holes in it, one block at a time and paced, keeping the lowest free space written for new data. A database whose data shrank after large deletes or TTL expiry then occupies about its live data on disk (`du`), though its length (`ls -l`) stays the same. Only space that nothing can still read or recover is punched. New metrics: `file_punch_passes`, `file_punches`, `file_punch_bytes`, `file_punched_bytes` and `file_punch_refusals`.
