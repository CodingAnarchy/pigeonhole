### Fixed
- **A cross-shard transaction no longer commits over a read whose tablet moved during its commit** (D145 amended). When a read's tablet moved to another participant of the commit after that participant had validated its own share, no shard validated the read, and the transaction could commit over a value written since its snapshot. A participant that handed a tablet away since the commit was routed now refuses such a read, and the commit runs again through the new tablet map. #2054's test of a tablet change waiting for a share's read is deterministic now: its race showed this in CI.
