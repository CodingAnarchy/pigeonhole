### Fixed
- A replica promote (`phdb cluster promote`, or the map writer's failover) that stopped between making the copy a primary and writing its identity at the new epoch can now be finished (#2156). Before, every rerun refused the file as one the map had moved past. The node now records the promote's intent beside the file before promoting it, and removes it once the copy is stamped. A rerun that finds the intent only writes the identity. The map's writer also resumes a pending promote whose copy hasn't been stamped yet. A dropped partition's files take the intent with them.
