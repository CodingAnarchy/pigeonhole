### Fixed
- **A spanning transaction's committed decision row no longer stays for ever when a branch's ack is lost** (#2098). A lost ack is one owed from a branch whose node restarted while it was owed, or the home's own record of a branch that answered its decide round failing. Once such a row is past `--txn-outcome-retention`, the home's decision sweep sends each branch that never acknowledged the commit its decide again. A branch that answers holds nothing of it in doubt (a share still in doubt is applied then), so its ack is recorded and the row is collected. A branch whose node doesn't answer keeps the row until it does.
