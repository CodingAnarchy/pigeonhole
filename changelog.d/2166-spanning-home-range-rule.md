### Fixed
- **A spanning transaction's home commit could write into a range that had been handed over, and the write was lost** (#2155). A plain commit and a spanning branch's prepare were refused across a range move's fence, but the home's own commit wasn't. A transaction homed on the source that buffered a row of the moving range before the fence could commit it there after the hand-over and be answered committed; the target never got the row. The home now applies the same rule: its commit is refused (`Fenced` or `Conflict`, retry-safe, nothing written) when it's open across the range's fence or hand-over, and it takes no row of a fenced or moved-away range.
