### Fixed
- **The balancer no longer leaves a partition closed when it can't unfence it after aborting an earlier writer's move** (#2151). The abort removed the move's record and then sent one unfence to the partition's node. If that failed (the node didn't answer just then, or its peers hadn't confirmed the map yet), nothing tried again, and the partition stayed fenced and closed until an operator ran `phdb cluster unfence`. The balancer now keeps the unfence, tries it again each pass and decides nothing else until the partition serves; `balancer status` names the partition and the error meanwhile. The retry is in the writer's memory and lifts only the fence that move set: after a writer restart or a change of writer, `phdb cluster status` still shows the partition closed on its old node, and the unfence is by hand.
