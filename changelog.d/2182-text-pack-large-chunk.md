### Fixed
- **`Index::pack` no longer stalls at a chunk too large for one commit** (#2182). A chunk of a term's postings whose transaction was refused for size (`BatchTooLarge` or `RecordTooLarge`: a common word in very long documents, or a small `memtable_budget`) failed the pack with its cursor unmoved, so every later pack, and every `--text-pack` pass, failed at the same place and never packed the terms after it. Searches stayed right. Such a chunk is now retried smaller, down to one posting, as a conflicted one is, and the rest of that term's chunks start at the size that fit. A posting written while the database was opened with a larger `memtable_budget` than now can still stop the pack at that term: reopen with the larger budget to pack it. The guides now also say that an index left unpacked grows with deletes, and that a pack can end with a `Conflict`-coded error.
