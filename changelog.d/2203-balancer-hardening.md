### Fixed
- **The cluster balancer no longer counts a partition it has no numbers for as empty and idle** (#2158). A partition the map places on a node that answered, but which is closed, opening or not open there, counted as 0 bytes and 0 load. Its node looked lighter and colder (a move target), and the partition small and cold (a merge candidate). Such a node now sits the pass out, as one that didn't answer does, and the balancer's `state` names the partition.
- **A balancer move no longer takes its target over its size bound to even out load, or over its load bound to even out size** (#2158). Only the measure the source was over on was checked, so once the dwell ended a move took the partition back. The target is now the coldest node that stays within both bounds.
- **`phdb cluster balancer status` no longer says "within bounds" while a node is over its bound** (#2158). A node, or a partition too big or too hot, still counting its passes or resting now says so ("node a is over its bound (1.43x the mean size): 4 of 10 passes so far", "… but rests 1200 s more").
