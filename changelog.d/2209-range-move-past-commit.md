### Fixed
- **Range moves: a node down after a move's hand-over no longer wedges it, or holds off failover for good** (#2154). Past the commit point the move goes on with the nodes that answer (the source's lease ending on a majority takes it to CLEANING). A failover or promote of the source may go ahead from HANDED_OVER: the promoted copy never serves the handed-over range and drops its copy at CLEANING. A failover or promote of the target goes only to a replica copy whose own progress rows show the move's copy done and caught up past its last seqno; `phdb cluster status` reports those for each replica copy. At HANDING_OVER both partitions are still held until the move commits or aborts.
