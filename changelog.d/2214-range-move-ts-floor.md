### Fixed
- **A write to a range just moved is no longer hidden behind the cells the move copied** (#2153). A range move's target (`move-range`, `split`, `merge` and the balancer's splits and merges) wrote each copied cell at the source's timestamp but never raised its own timestamp floor past them. So when its clock ran behind the source's, or the source's floor ran ahead of its clock, a put or delete on the target after the hand-over could take an older timestamp than the copied cell. Reads then returned the old value until the target's clock passed it. The target now raises its floor past every cell it copied or caught up, before it serves the range. A move whose copy began on a 1.3.0 node counts only what it copied after the upgrade, so finish or abort moves before the rolling upgrade.
