### Added
- `phdb serve` clusters: a partition's replica copies acknowledge what they've applied to its owner (D335's first part, #2149, #2236). A copy follows through the new `Replication.FollowAcked`, falling back to `Follow` on an owner from an older build, and acknowledges each applied run once it's durable. `Cluster.Status` reports, on the owner, `LocalPartition.acked_position` and `healthy_replicas`, and on a replica copy `replica_applied_position`. The owner's `/metrics` adds `pigeonhole_cluster_replica_acked_seqno{partition,replica}`, and `pigeonhole_cluster_replica_ack_wait_seconds{partition}`, from a write's commit to the first replica's acknowledgement. No write waits for acknowledgements yet.
