### Added
- `phdb serve` clusters: semi-synchronous replication (D335's second part, #2149, #2238). A partition's map entry takes `replica_acks` (k, 0 by default); with k at 1 or more, a write on it is answered only once k of its replicas have acknowledged it, and `CommitInfo.replica_acks` says how many did. A write still waiting at `--cluster-replica-ack-timeout-ms` (default 5000), or when its partition's lease ends, answers `OutcomeUnknown`. While fewer than k replicas are healthy, writes are refused with `Unavailable`, retry-safe. A write opts out with `replication: REPLICATION_MODE_ASYNC` (on `Mutate`, `MutateReturning`, `CheckAndMutate`, `Batch` and a transaction's `Commit`) or the `x-pigeonhole-replication: async` header. A write that waits has `None` or `Buffered` durability raised to `GroupSync`. A map sets k only once every node runs a build with it. `/metrics` adds `pigeonhole_cluster_replica_ack_timeouts_total`, `_lease_lost_total`, `_short_seconds_total` and `_refused_total` per partition. Spanning transactions don't wait yet.
