### Added
- **A promote or failover of a cluster partition records the old owner's unreplicated tail** (#2148, D336). The copy's fork point is kept through the promote (beside the promote's intent, then in the partition file with its identity) and recorded in the failover event (`fork_token`, `tail_state` PENDING), so a later release can replay the old owner's commits into the new owner. While a tail is pending, the partition's moves and history changes are refused, and the balancer leaves it alone. At most 4 pending tails are kept per partition, the oldest discarded. Recorded only once the cluster adopted `tail_replay` (`adopted_features`); otherwise the promote warns and goes on.
