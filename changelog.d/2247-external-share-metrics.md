### Added
- **Metrics for transactions across partitions** (#2221, ICR 0148). The engine counts the shares it prepares for them and the decides it takes, to commit or abort (`EngineMetrics::external_prepares`, `external_commits` and `external_aborts`; `pigeonhole_txn_external_prepares_total` and `pigeonhole_txn_external_decides_total{pigeonhole_outcome}`). It also counts how often change-feed readers held such a share until its decision, and let one go at their read-ahead limit (`feed_external_held` and `feed_external_evicted`; `pigeonhole_feed_external_holds_total` and `pigeonhole_feed_external_evictions_total`). On a cluster node, `phdb serve` shows what each partition holds in doubt now: `pigeonhole_cluster_txn_in_doubt_shares{partition}` and `pigeonhole_cluster_txn_in_doubt_wal_bytes{partition}`.
