### Changed
- **A partition's device load rises smoothly from 0, and an idle cluster's load noise moves nothing** (#2208). `load_device` is now the partition's rate over the larger of its node's ceiling and 32 MiB/s. In 1.3.1 it read 0 under 1 MiB/s and the whole share just over it, so a trickle at 1.1 MiB/s could read as hot. A node whose device has run faster than 32 MiB/s reads exactly as before. The balancer counts a node as over its load bound only when its load is also over a tenth of `partition_max_load` (0.05 at the default), so an idle cluster's uneven trickles don't move partitions. A target whose load stays under that after a move isn't refused for it on load. `phdb cluster status` and the load-ratio metric still show loads and ratios as measured.
