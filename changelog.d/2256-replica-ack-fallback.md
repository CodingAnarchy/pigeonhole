### Added
- `phdb serve` clusters: a `replica_acks` partition's `ASYNC` policy (D335's third part, #2149, #2256). A partition's map entry takes `replica_ack_policy`: `REFUSE` (the default, and 1.4.0's earlier behaviour) refuses writes while too few replicas are healthy; `ASYNC` takes them and answers them without their replica acknowledgements (`CommitInfo.replica_acks` below k), once a majority of the cluster's nodes have recorded on disk where that fallback began, and ends it, again recorded, once its replicas hold every write answered so. Each node keeps those records in `DIR/cluster-degraded.json` (a file that doesn't parse stops the node's start), and `Status` shows them (`NodeStatus.degraded`) and the owner's start (`LocalPartition.degraded_from`). `/metrics` adds `pigeonhole_cluster_replica_ack_degraded` (gauge) and `_degraded_total` per partition. A map sets the policy only once every node runs a build with it. A spanning transaction's prepare is also refused on a `replica_acks` partition's own node unless its commit opted down (`Conflict`, retry-safe); during a rolling upgrade, one driven by an older node is refused there even when it opted down.
