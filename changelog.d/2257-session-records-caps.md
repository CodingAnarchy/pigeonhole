### Fixed
- **`phdb serve`: a committed transaction no longer holds a session slot for `--txn-idle-ms`** (#2253). Its record (kept so a repeated `Commit` answers its outcome), and an expired transaction's marker, counted against `--max-sessions-per-token` and `--max-sessions` for 30 s, which capped one token (or a server without tokens) at about 2 commits a second. They now count against a per-node bound of their own, 131,072 records, the oldest dropped first (`pigeonhole_serve_session_records_dropped_total`); a `Commit` retried after its record was dropped answers `NotFound`.
