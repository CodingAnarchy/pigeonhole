### Fixed
- **`phdb serve`: a client past `--max-connections` is told so, rather than seeing a bare close** (#2262). Since 1.2.0, a connection past the cap was closed before anything was read. A gRPC client then failed its calls with `Unknown` or `Cancelled`, not the documented `UNAVAILABLE`, and reconnected for every call. Now and then a call waited until another call on the same channel woke it. Now the server answers each request on such a connection with `Overloaded`: gRPC gets `UNAVAILABLE` ("the server is at --max-connections"), and HTTP/1.1 gets a 503 with `Connection: close`. It holds the connection a second at most (`--request-timeout-ms` if shorter), and HTTP/2 then sends `GOAWAY`. At most 32 connections are refused this way at once; past those, a connection is still closed at once. `pigeonhole_serve_connections_refused_total` counts both.
