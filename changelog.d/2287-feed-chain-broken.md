### Fixed
- **Change feed: a feed stuck after a broken WAL segment chain now fails, rather than waiting forever** (#2279). When a reader's segment had no successor on disk though a later segment was there, its feed returned nothing more and raised no error, at every poll. A zeroed successor header (from the truncation in #2279) left it that way, and so did retention recycling the next segment while the reader's own was not yet reused. Now the first case fails the feed with `Corruption` ("wal segment chain broken at epoch N"), after one more look at the headers. The second expires it, as any feed retention passed: subscribe again from a newer token.
