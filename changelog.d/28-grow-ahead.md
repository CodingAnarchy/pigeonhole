### Changed
- The page file grows ahead of need: once an allocation leaves no free 64 MiB block in a file of at least 128 MiB, the file grows by one such block without holding the allocator lock. The `fallocate` runs on the allocating thread and the `sync_all` is submitted to the I/O backend, and root commits wait for its outcome as for any growth sync (#182, D58). Before, the flush or compaction step that ran out of space grew the file inline, with the allocator lock held across the `fallocate` and `sync_all` (35–52 ms per growth on the Phase 3 reference machine, blocking every other allocation). An inline growth now happens only while a growth ahead is still in flight, or below 128 MiB. `PagerStats` gains `growths_ahead` and `ahead_nanos`, engine `Metrics` gains `file_growths_ahead`, and the bench's stall counters gain `file_growths_ahead`.
