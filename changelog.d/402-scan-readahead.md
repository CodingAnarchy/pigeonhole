### Changed
- Scan readahead (#402): on a forward-scan cache miss, `pigeonhole-sst` cursors with `ReadOptions::readahead_blocks = N` now submit fetches for up to N of the next blocks the cache lacks (`SstIter::upcoming`, the one predictor shared with async scans, ICR 0017), admitting each as it completes, and read on meanwhile. A block reached while its fetch is in flight is waited for, not read again. The contiguous single-read readahead (`read_run`) is gone. `pigeonhole_sst::counting_readahead` reports fetches issued, used and waited for. Engine scans keep N = 4; `PIGEONHOLE_READAHEAD=N[,merge]` overrides it for the gate measurement (#431).
