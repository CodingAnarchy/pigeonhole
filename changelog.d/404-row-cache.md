### Added
- **The row cache works** (#404, D201, ICR 0019). `Options::row_cache(bytes)` used to do nothing. It now serves latest row reads of the newest version and point gets from cached family rows, without merging the row's sources. Results are identical to uncached reads: a write to a row makes its cached copy miss at once, and a cell's TTL expiry does too. New: `Options::row_cache_max_row` (the per-row cap, default 4 KiB), `Options::row_cache_family` (serve only the listed families), and `Pigeonhole::row_cache_stats`. It's off by default. Snapshot reads, multi-version and time-range reads, scans and reader processes don't use it.
- `pigeonhole-cache`: `RowEpochs`, the per-row write watermarks the row cache's epochs come from. `pigeonhole-engine`: `EngineOptions::row_cache_max_row` and `row_cache_families`, `Engine::row_cache_stats`, `RowCacheStats`, and `RowSink::cell_count` and `cell` (provided methods).
