### Changed
- **In application-owned mode, `Shard::run_once` returns early when it completed a future woken through `Shard::waker()` (D237).** A call that settles a commit the driving thread awaits now returns `true` right after the shard's queued work, before its background work (flush, compaction), so the loop polls the commit sooner. Background work runs on the next call, and at the latest on every ninth. A loop should poll its futures after every `run_once` call, not only once it returns `false`. The async guide's loop now does. On the reference machine's 16-shard durable group commit this lowers the p99 by about 20 µs at 16 commits in flight and about 30 µs at 8.
