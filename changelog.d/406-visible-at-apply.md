### Changed
- **A commit is visible to readers once applied, not once durable (D227, amends D19).** A shard publishes a group's seqnos once its WAL records are handed to the OS and it is applied, without waiting for the group's fsync. A commit's own call still returns only once it is durable at its level and visible, and cross-shard commits are unchanged. Readers, reader processes and backups can therefore see `GroupSync` and `Sync` commits that a power loss then removes. Across shards, a later commit can survive an earlier one it was derived from. A seqno or default timestamp a reader saw can be handed out again after a power loss. The durability guide's "Visibility and durability" section has examples. A durable snapshot for readers that need the old view follows (ICR 0037).

### Added
- **`ErrorCode::OutcomeUnknown` (28) and `Error::OutcomeUnknown` (ICR 0038).** A commit whose outcome is not known in this process now fails with this error. That happens when a shard failed or the database closed while the commit was in flight, after its records may have reached the log. Reopen and read: the next open decides the commit, all of it or none of it. `Closed` now always means the commit was not committed. A visibility wait that ends because the database closed or a shard died now returns `OutcomeUnknown`, not `Closed`.

### Fixed
- A close no longer hangs when a shard dies in the middle of a cross-shard commit. If the coordinator died, a participant stops waiting for that share's decision. If a participant died, the coordinator stops waiting for its reply: a cross-shard commit still undecided is aborted (`Closed`), and one already decided fails with `OutcomeUnknown`. A decision that reaches a participant after its close gave up is not applied. The commit's seqno then stays unpublished, so no reader sees part of it. In each case the close is unclean, and the next open decides the share from its COMMIT record.
