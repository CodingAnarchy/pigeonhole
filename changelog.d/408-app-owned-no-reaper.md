### Changed
- In application-owned mode with `IoBackend::Uring`, the engine starts no threads (#408): the shared io_uring ring (for threads without a ring of their own, such as async reads on an executor) has no reaper thread. The threads that drive shards reap it, woken through their `Shard::io_fd` when it has completions, and a thread blocked on one of its completions reaps it itself. `pigeonhole-io` adds `UringVfs::new_application_owned`. In that mode, wait on `Shard::io_fd` in the event loop: an async read submitted from a thread that drives no shard otherwise completes only at the loop's next timed turn (see the async guide).
