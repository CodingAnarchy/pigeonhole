### Changed
- Durable group commits share their syncs (#412, D207): a shard keeps a bounded number of group syncs in flight per WAL stream (1 on macOS, 2 elsewhere, until the gate run decides; `PIGEONHOLE_GROUP_SYNC_DEPTH` overrides it for measurement), and commits that need one meanwhile batch behind them and share the next. At 16 clients: Linux 29.5K → about 31.5K ops/s with p99 1.46 → about 1.0 ms; macOS 578 → 2.2K ops/s with p99 33 → 11.6 ms. Before, each small group started its own sync even while one was running, so at 16 concurrent clients a sync covered about 4.5 commits.
