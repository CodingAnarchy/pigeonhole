### Added
- **Scan streams** (#42, D196; behind `async`): `Scan::stream` is a `futures_core::Stream` of owned `Row`s. Each tablet is positioned without blocking, and the blocks each step will read are fetched asynchronously first, one block ahead and only as the stream is polled. A block a step reads that was not predicted is read synchronously and counted in `async_sync_reads` (resumable steps are #398). The `async` feature now depends on `futures-core`.
- `pigeonhole-sst`: `SstIter::set_cache_only`, `upcoming(max, out)` (the one prediction of what a scan reads next, which the sync readahead hint builds on) and `at_last_block`, `SstReader::first_fetch`, and `counting_file_reads` (ICR 0017). `pigeonhole-engine`: `Engine::scan_async`, `ScanCursor::poll_next_row` and `counted`.
