### Added
- With `IoBackend::Uring`, each ring registers a pool of buffer slots with the kernel on the first read that can use it (fixed buffers, #402): reads into them skip pinning their pages. `pigeonhole-io` adds `File::read_buf` (a buffer for a submitted read: a registered slot where the backend has one) and `IoBuf::detached` (copies a slot-backed buffer to the heap when it is kept beyond its I/O); the block cache stores detached buffers. A process registers pools up to half its locked-memory limit (`RLIMIT_MEMLOCK`; `CAP_IPC_LOCK` is exempt), up to 1 MiB a ring; `Pigeonhole::io_rings` (and `PigeonholeReader::io_rings`) reports how many rings got one, and `Vfs::ring_stats` (default `None`) is the backend's side.
