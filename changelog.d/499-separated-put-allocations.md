### Changed
- A separated large put allocates less on its way to the blob file and the manifest (#499): 92 allocations per 1 MiB put instead of 113 in the allocation-budget test (about 44 of them are the simulated file's own copies), and a flush's manifest commit about 10 fewer. Each blob file's extent list is reserved once rather than grown extent by extent. The catalog's blob files share their extent lists, so a commit that adds a blob file no longer copies every other file's list. The manifest writer reuses its block buffer between commits. A manifest block's checksum no longer boxes a copy of the hash secret. `pigeonhole-sst` adds `BlobWriter::reserve_for`.
