### Fixed
- After a crash, a reopened WAL stream could bring back records that recovery had not seen (#508, D209). When a segment's successor had its records but not its header on disk, the reopened stream took the lost header's epoch in the same recycled slot, so those records replayed at the next recovery and their seqnos were reused by new commits. On 0.2.0 this needed a power loss during the write that starts a segment in a recycled slot. Since #19 (D200), a process kill during a rollover could also cause it, and so could a power loss that lost two segments' headers at once. Recovery now starts a reopened stream three epochs above the largest seen (FORMAT §10.1 rule 2). Files written before the fix recover unchanged.
