### Fixed
- Cross-shard commits under contention were refused and retried several times each, because a participant compared a PREPARE's timestamp against everything it had stamped since, in any order. On a 16-shard contended load the default configuration ran about 36 times slower than with tablet changes off. The check is now exact by seqno, and the coordinator raises each participant's timestamp floor before it reserves the commit's seqno, so the participant's later writes always land above the commit (D245, #637).
- A cross-shard commit whose share waited for memtable room on a participant could hold back the durable watermark that the participant's freeze needed, so room never freed (#655). The share is now refused and retried once the participant has freed room (D245).
