### Fixed
- A cross-shard commit could get an earlier default timestamp than a later-seqno write on one of its shards, so a later write could be hidden by an earlier one on the same cell (D11). A single commit queued on a shard behind a cross-shard commit's PREPARE could land below it, and with tablet changes off a share could land below its shard's earlier writes. Admitted PREPAREs now raise the shard's timestamp floor before the group's singles are stamped, and a PREPARE out of seqno order with a write its shard already stamped is refused and retried, with tablet changes on or off (D244, #653). Cross-shard commits are refused and retried more often under contention as a result. A retry that can't commit before the write-stall timeout fails with `Busy`, as before.
