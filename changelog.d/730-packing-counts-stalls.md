### Fixed
- Tablet packing now counts the time a shard's commits spend waiting for memtable room or paced on L0 depth as busy, beside the shard thread's work time and its fsync utilization (D255). With flush and compaction on their own threads (D252) a shard under a full write load read idle, so packing kept whole tables on one shard: on a 16 GiB random load one shard held 86 tablets on one arena, flushed sub-megabyte memtables by the thousand, and the load wrote 45× its data and took 6× longer than with flush and compaction on the shards. The first shrink from the unpacked start now waits until the store has written for a full slow-average window (30 s), a shrink count does not advance while the load ramps and resets on a stalling pass, and a stall-driven growth repeats while the packed shard has tablets to shed (#734): a load's phases no longer pack the store on the dip between them and move every tablet back out during the measurement.
