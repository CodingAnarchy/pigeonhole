### Fixed
- A shard popped every record its WAL checkpoint could pass in one handler run. After a flush report, a settle or a flush install that freed thousands of records, that held a turn for 3–5 ms, and the shard's pending watermark with it. On the AX102's cross-shard row this happened about five times per shard per second and caused the 0.4–0.5 ms p99.9 of its packing-off row. A batch now pops at most 64 records and the rest follow on the next batches; a closing shard still drains its log in one go. An aborted cross-shard share also no longer runs a checkpoint pass unless its record is the oldest in the log (#763).
