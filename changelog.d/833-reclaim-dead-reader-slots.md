### Fixed
- The writer now frees the slot of a reader process that has exited or was killed (#833). A crashed reader's pin used to hold the writer's retired memtables, view versions and extents until the next writer started, so a long-running writer could end up failing commits with `Busy`. The writer checks reader liveness at most every 250 ms, before each reclamation. A live reader's slot is never freed, provided readers share the writer's PID namespace (the existing requirement). On Linux, a reader that `/proc` hides (under `hidepid`, another user's process) now counts as alive. A reader wrongly judged dead gets `SnapshotExpired` and a fresh slot at its next snapshot, never wrong data or another reader's slot.
