### Fixed
- With `Options::auto_recover`, a commit the failed engine refused before logging it could fail with `Io` instead of `Closed` (#966): after the supervisor gave up (`recovery_stats().gave_up`), when it reached the failed shard just before the database was marked dead; and for async commits and commit tickets, which do not wait, during a recovery too. Each now gets `Closed`, as the recovery guide says; it was not committed either way.
