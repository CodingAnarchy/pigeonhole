### Fixed
- With `Options::auto_recover`, a commit the failed engine refused before logging it could fail with `Io` (#966): after the supervisor gave up (`recovery_stats().gave_up`), when it reached the failed shard just before the database was marked dead; and for async commits and commit tickets, which do not wait, during a recovery too. A sync commit, an async one and a ticket now all get what the recovery guide says: `Busy` once the recovered engine is in (`SnapshotExpired` for a transaction), `Closed` while the recovery runs or after a give-up. None of them was committed.
