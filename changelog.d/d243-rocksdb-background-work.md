### Changed
- The bench reports RocksDB's background work (D233), so `phdb-bench compare` can say when a RocksDB pair differs in the flushes and compactions inside its measured phases, as it already does for Pigeonhole. The count is the sum of `Comp(cnt)` in each column family's `Sum` row of `rocksdb.stats`; statistics stay off. The optimistic transaction runner (the judged `txn=optimistic` baseline) now reports background work at all, so its settle step waits for its background work to stop before the warmup, as every other store's does. The pessimistic runner still reports none: its `TransactionDB` exposes properties only in multi-threaded mode. Pigeonhole's numbers are unchanged.
