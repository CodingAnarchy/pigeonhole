### Changed
- **The bench settles again after the warmup (D243's amendment of 2026-10-29).** D233's settle step (flush, then wait for background work to stop) now runs after the warmup too, so every client run's measured phase starts on an empty write buffer. A flush inside the measured phase lands at the same operation in both runs of a reproducibility pair; before, the warmup's length decided whether it landed there or in the warmup, which moved `adjacency`'s p50 between about 12 and 17 µs. The JSON records the step as `detail.settle_after_warmup`, and a traced run marks a `warmup-settle` phase. **This changes the gate's methodology: every `all` and `contended` number moves to a new baseline.** The inline drivers are unchanged.
