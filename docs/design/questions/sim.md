## Proposed decision: the model's per-stream recovery helper lives in `pigeonhole-sim` (#40)
`pigeonhole-sim` offers `StreamCommit`, `recovered_commits`, `check_acknowledged_survive` and `Model::from_commits`: each stream keeps a prefix of its records, a single-shard commit survives iff its record does, and a cross-shard commit iff every PREPARE and its COMMIT do (D83). `Model::crash_window` stays as the single-stream special case (D84). D42's heading already says "per WAL stream", so no wording change is needed. The engine and public-crate suites adopt the helper in a follow-up issue; until then the engine keeps its own rebuild in `crates/engine/tests/common`.
